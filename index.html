04 — FAQ

Straight answers

No fine print, no marketing fog

Because that's exactly what it does — you rip the media out of the feed and keep it. Verb, two syllables, no explanation needed. The dot is the period at the end of the argument.

The page you're on is the complete front-end. Browsers alone can't pull streams from YouTube or Instagram — ripr. ships with a tiny companion server (server.py, powered by the open-source yt-dlp engine). Run it on your machine and connect once with ?api=http://localhost:8000 — the badge flips to SERVER LIVE and every rip becomes a real file with real progress. Without it, everything runs in honest demo mode.

Personal, offline use is treated differently around the world — and both platforms' terms have opinions about it. Re-uploading or monetising someone else's work is never okay. Respect creators; don't be that person.

Yes — ripr. is a PWA. On any HTTPS host your browser will offer "Install". On Android you can even share a reel straight from Instagram's share sheet and ripr. opens with the link already loaded. Scan the QR code (top-right) to open it on your phone instantly.

No ad networks, no trackers, no redirect mazes. One HTML file, two fonts, zero frameworks. Your link goes in, the file comes out — that's the entire business model, because there isn't one.
